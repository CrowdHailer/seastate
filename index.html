About this location list

This is an extensive starting list, not a complete register of every UK beach or cove. Some long beaches have separate named sections. Counties and coastal areas are grouped for browsing, rather than as a strict administrative hierarchy. Inland rivers, reservoirs and lakes are excluded.

The core list uses Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales coastal and estuarine locations, SEPA coastal locations and Northern Ireland's coastal bathing locations, with additional well-known beaches. Names and groupings checked on 17 October 2026.

  • Natural Resources Wales
  • SEPA — Scotland
  • DAERA — Northern Ireland
